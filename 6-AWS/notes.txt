AWS Regions
    Choosing a Region is based on:
    - Compliance
    - Latency/ Proximity to customers
    - Pricing
    - Availablity of services

AWS Availablity Zones:
    Discrete data centers with redundant power, networking and connectivity

Edge Locations (PoP):
    Infrastructure used by services such as CloudFront and Route53

Global services:
    IAM , CloudFront (CDN) , Web App Firewall (WAF) , Route 53 (DNS)

Region scoped services: 
    https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/global-infrastructure/regional-product-services/
    - Not all services are available in all Regions
